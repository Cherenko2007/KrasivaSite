Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how KRASIVA aesthetics studio collects, uses and protects the personal data of website users.

We may collect the following data:

  • Name and contact phone number;

  • Information about preferred services and desired booking time;
  • Other information you voluntarily provide when communicating with the master.

2. Purposes of data processing

Personal data is used exclusively for:

  • Booking appointments and confirming visits;

3. Disclosure to third parties

We do not transfer your personal data to third parties without your consent, except as required by Russian law.

4. Data storage and security

Data is stored securely. Access is limited to the studio master. We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorised access.

5. Your rights

You have the right to request the deletion or correction of your personal data at any time by sending a message via Instagram or Telegram.
