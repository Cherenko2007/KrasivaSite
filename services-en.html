Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any contraindications? ▼

Contraindications apply only to vibration massage: pregnancy and oncology.

For all other types (classic, anti-cellulite, blade, face, back, neck) there are no contraindications.

How should I prepare for a session? ▼

It's best to come with clean skin, avoid heavy meals 1–2 hours before. Drink water before and after massage – it helps flush out toxins. If you have any medical certificates or doctor's recommendations, bring them along.

How many sessions are needed for visible results? ▼

Results are usually noticeable after 3–5 sessions. For lasting effects, a course of 10–12 procedures is recommended. It depends on individual characteristics and goals – we'll create a plan at the first consultation.

Is the massage painful? ▼

Massage should not cause sharp pain. You may feel pleasant warmth and tension in the worked areas, but not pain. We always adjust pressure according to your feelings – you should be comfortable.

How do I book a session? ▼

You can book via the form on the website, by writing to Instagram (@natamirmat) or VKontakte. We'll find a convenient time and answer all your questions.
